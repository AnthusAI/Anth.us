## What this article owns

The 2026 fact that used to live in cost-collapse, said as an operator rule:

- "Fast" used to mean cheap (Haiku, Flash, mini).
- "Fast" now means a more expensive version of a bigger model (Fable 5, Sol Ultrafast). Extra iron. You pay to wait less.
- **Never use fast.** Not "prefer cheap." A ban, with one honest exception sentence (incident, live pairing, maybe the architectural pass).

Then the practical list. Not another chart of fourteen models. Buttons and defaults:

- Cursor: do not use Auto / Auto Balance / Auto Intelligence. Pin Composer 2.5 or Luna (or the current cheap workhorse). Auto is willing to spend more.

### Cursor: Grok as manager for Composer

Grok is a pretty good manager for Composer. At time of writing: Grok 4.6 as the manager for a parallel team of Composer 2.5 agents. Other models (Opus, Sol, GLM 5.2) still collaborate in the mix. Composer does the real work. Grok orchestrates.

Turn Fast off. Composer Fast is about 6× Composer. Grok 4.6 is about 4× Composer 2.5. Grok also burns more tokens. It is still an economical way to get smart management of a high-value coding agent.

Plan with Grok. Part of the plan must be: implementation is Composer 2.5 sub-agents. Tell it that before you click implement. If you click "yes, implement the plan" without that in the plan, it will not build Composer 2.5 into the plan.

Notes, not finished how-to copy. Recheck the 6× / 4× multipliers before publish.

- Codex: do not accept the picker defaults. The tool hides the cheap Codex models and the `gpt-5.6` alias routes to Sol. Re-add the affordable ones by hand.
- Anthropic: do not pick Fable for everyday work. Opus is already the expensive flagship; Fable is that plus a speed tax.
- Never buy Ultrafast / "priority" / "fast" modes.
- Start N cheap sessions so you are never staring at one spinner. Parallel is how patience stays cheap.
- Scale out across projects. You are the manager. That is tiring and it is the job.

Audience: you paying out of pocket, and you at a job where someone is watching the bill.
