# Idea

## Pitch

Lead with the fun, mean line: they offer you the new Fast feature like a drug dealer giving you a free first hit, except it isn't even free. Token companies keep finding levers to sell you more tokens, and more-expensive tokens. Interests are misaligned. They don't want you to notice. You are your own advocate. Just say no.

Then a list of specific settings. The general principle is Resist temptation. Never Use Fast owns the Fast tax table. This piece owns the rest of the catalog, and the adversarial frame Fast sits inside.
